# Prose Rubric

Review in descending priority:

1. Scientific correctness and evidence alignment.
2. Section purpose and paper-level argument.
3. Paragraph claim, support, interpretation, and boundary.
4. Terminology, tense, logical transitions, and sentence clarity.
5. Grammar, mechanics, and concision.

Critical findings include fabricated or unsupported claims, changed numerical meaning, false citation attribution, and causal or optimality claims that exceed validation. Major findings include missing gap, mixed results and interpretation, absent limitations, and paragraphs without evidence. Minor findings include repetition, awkward transitions, inconsistent terms, and local grammar.
