# Audit Rubric

`Critical` defects force FAIL:

- missing or invalid final source/PDF;
- central number without traceable computation;
- fabricated or unverifiable citation;
- unresolved contradiction between model, code, results, and prose;
- failed required validation or competition-rule violation;
- unreadable, clipped, blank, or materially broken PDF content.

`Major` defects require repair before PASS when they affect interpretation:

- weak method justification, missing units, misleading chart, stale PDF, incomplete limitations, or broken cross-reference.

`Minor` defects are corrected when feasible and may remain only if they do not affect correctness or compliance:

- isolated wording, spacing, caption, or bibliography-style issues.

Every finding includes severity, artifact, location, evidence, repair, and recheck status.
